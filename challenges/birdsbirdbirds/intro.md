Every bird in the sky started as a doodle. Yours is next.

You will:

- Draw a bird on a small canvas in your browser.
- Tell Pi who your bird is, where it flies in from, and how it
  should fly, in your own words.
- Watch Pi sketch the flight on the sky as a numbered path, then
  direct it: "add a loop at 3", "slower at the end".
- Send your finished bird to join the flock on the booth screens.

Any bird works: realistic, abstract, silly, or a scribble with wings.
Keep the name and origin family-friendly, because your bird flies on
the booth screens. You can take it down from the station anytime.
