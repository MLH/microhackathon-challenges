Every bird in the sky started as a doodle. Yours is next.

You will:

- Draw a bird on a small canvas in your browser.
- Tell Pi who your bird is, where it flies in from, and how it moves.
- Pi writes the flight as a CSS animation, and a checker makes sure
  it is safe before your bird takes off.
- Watch your bird fly across the sky.

Any bird works: realistic, abstract, silly, or a scribble with wings.
Keep the name and origin family-friendly, because birds can appear on
the booth screens.

At the end, Pi asks if you want your bird shown there.
