# Local Vibe Coding

Fill in four tab-navigable fields to tell Pi what to build. Invent your answers,
or roll a d6, d6, d20, and d20 and use the matching entries on the optional
[`dice-sheet.md`](dice-sheet.md). You can mix the two approaches.

Pi will make one interactive HTML file. Open its localhost URL, try it, then
ask Pi for one change and try the result. Nothing is published or saved to an
online gallery. Your submitted idea goes to the configured model; the app and
Pi conversation remain in local scratch storage after exit. Do not enter
secrets. This takes about 10 minutes and is worth 100 points only when
run through a future boothstrap station; standalone Pi does not award points.
