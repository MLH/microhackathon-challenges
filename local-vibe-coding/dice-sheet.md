# Optional dice sheet

Roll d6 for Theme, d6 for Subject, d20 for Audience, and d20 for Style. Copy
the four results into Pi's editable prompt. You can also invent any answer or
mix your own answers with rolled ones. The options match Vibecannon's booth
tables; this activity does not roll dice automatically.
