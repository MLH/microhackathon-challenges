# Hello, World

Meet Pi, your AI guide. You'll ask it to read a welcome note and then make a
small text file for you. No terminal experience needed.

1. Ask Pi to read `welcome.txt` and say hello.
2. Ask Pi to create `hello.txt` containing `Hello, world!`.

You'll practice giving an agent a clear instruction and checking its work.
This takes about 5 minutes and earns 50 points when you finish both steps.
