# Four blanks, one web app

Tell Pi what to build by filling in one sentence:

> Build a [Theme] about [Subject] for [Audience] in [Style] style.

A form opens after Pi says hello. Type your own answers, or roll the dice at
the booth and look up each roll on `dice-sheet.md`. The dice are optional,
and mixing rolled and invented answers is fine.

Pi then builds your app as one web page and offers to open it in Chrome beside
the station. Try it, ask Pi for one change, and try it again.

Your sentence and requests go to the Gemini model, so leave out anything
private. Everything here is wiped when you leave.
