Tell Pi what to build by filling in one sentence:

> Build a [Theme] about [Subject] for [Audience] in [Style] style.

A form opens after Pi says hello. Type your own answers, or roll the dice
at the booth: press Esc and ask Pi to show `dice-sheet.md` to look up each
roll, then type `/mad-libs` to return to the form. The dice are optional,
and mixing rolled and invented answers is fine.

Pi then builds your app as one web page and offers to open it in Chrome
beside the station. Try it, ask Pi for one change, and try it again.

Your sentence and requests go to the Gemini model, so leave out anything
private. Everything here is wiped when you leave.
